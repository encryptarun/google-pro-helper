Google AI Pro setup and trial extension (BlueStacks Air on macOS)
==================================================

SUPPORTED PLAN
  Google AI Pro (5 TB) only:
    Step 1: 12-month free trial
    Step 2: Add 6 months
    Step 3: Add another 6 months (24 months total)
    Extra: Add another 6 months (up to 30 months total)

SET UP THE ENVIRONMENT (ONCE)
  Double-click setup_environment.command. It will:
    1. Check Python (3.10 or later).
    2. Match the Mac Frida client to the MagiskFrida server and install the Java bridge.
    3. Use BlueStacks Air ADB at /Applications/BlueStacks.app/Contents/MacOS/hd-adb.
    4. Connect to BlueStacks Air and check root, Frida, and Play Store access.
  Continue when you see "Environment check passed."

BEFORE YOU START
  1. BlueStacks Air is running with ADB access and root enabled.
     The target Google account is signed in. Stock installations may need
     separate root setup; this project does not modify BlueStacks itself.
  2. The account has a payment method (card).
  3. The account has no existing Google One subscription.
  4. Play Store opens normally. Restart the emulator if it does not.

DOUBLE-CLICK IN THIS ORDER
  0_environment_check.command          Check everything first.
  1_list_offers.command                View free offers for the current account;
                                   this does not start a purchase.

  2_pro_12_months_step_1.command       First purchase: 12 months free.
  3_pro_add_6_months_step_2.command    Second purchase: +6 months (18 total).
  4_pro_add_6_months_step_3.command    Third purchase: +6 months (24 total).
  5_pro_extra_6_months.command         Optional, after the first three succeed.

HOW THE EXTENSION WORKS
  A regular purchase replaces the same plan without extending its end date.
  The Play subscription upgrade flow is required:

    1. Read the current subscription's purchaseToken from Play's library.db.
       The script does this automatically; you do not need to enter the token.
    2. Set BillingFlowParams.SubscriptionUpdateParams:
         .setOldPurchaseToken(existing token)
         .setReplaceProrationMode(5)      // CHARGE_FULL_PRICE
    3. Open checkout again.

  A successful extension can look like this at checkout:

    Upcoming charge today: JP¥0
    546 days (including 364 days prorated from your current plan)
    Start date: May 18, 2028
    JP¥2,900/month
    Your first charge will be on May 18, 2028.

  If the start date is the current expiry date and a charge is due that day,
  the upgrade flow was not used. Check that the subscription is active and
  that the command includes --stack.

  Manual commands (equivalent to the .command launchers):
    .venv/bin/python pro.py --account "current@device" --no-add --force --plan pro12
    .venv/bin/python pro.py --account "current@device" --no-add --force --stack --plan pro6
    .venv/bin/python pro.py --account "current@device" --no-add --force --stack --plan pro6b
    .venv/bin/python pro.py --account "current@device" --no-add --force --stack --plan pro6c

SUBSCRIPTION STATUS AND EXTENSIONS
  The subscription must be active, with auto-renewal on, to extend it.
  If it has been canceled (Play shows "Canceled" or "Resubscribe"), a new
  purchase may become a paid plan after expiry instead of adding trial time.
  Check that it is active, apply all extensions, then cancel it.
  Canceling does not end the benefits before the expiry date.

VERIFY AFTER EACH STEP
  Open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions and check the next payment date:
    After step 1: about 12 months away
    After step 2: about 18 months away
    After step 3: about 24 months away
  Continue only if the date moves later. Stop if it does not.

TROUBLESHOOTING
  Stuck on "connecting"
    Play Store is not running. The script tries to start it; if that fails,
    open Play Store manually or restart the emulator.
  "You are already subscribed"
    This account already has that plan and cannot purchase it again.
  "No internet connection"
    The emulator briefly lost its connection. Wait a few seconds and retry;
    restart the emulator if it happens repeatedly.
  "Invalid signature"
    Web checkout does not accept the Android token. Use this script's
    Android Billing checkout flow.
  Price is not zero, or the date did not move later
    Do not confirm the purchase. Close checkout and check the subscription
    status and --stack option.

NOTES
  Repeated failures may trigger risk checks. Wait 24 hours before retrying.
  Cancel before expiry to avoid a JP¥2,900 monthly charge.
  Consider using a low-limit card in case you forget to cancel.
